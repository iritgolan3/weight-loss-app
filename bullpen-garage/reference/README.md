# Reference frames

Put screenshots from the source video here, named like `0m42s_door.jpg`, or
put the video itself here. The build environment could not download it:
YouTube is blocked by the network policy.

Good frames to capture:
1. The view through the open overhead door into the unit.
2. Wide shots that show both side walls, the mezzanine edge and the central post.
3. The stair, its rise direction and the guard style.
4. Each cabinet run, straight on, so modules can be counted.
5. The lounge, TV wall and bar.
6. The mezzanine furniture.
7. The floor finish, close up.
8. The ceiling, lights and structure.
9. Each car, side-on, with the location of every parked car.

With the frames here, refit `bullpen/config.py`. Known objects give the scale:
a 2,134 mm (7 ft) man door, a 4,267 mm (14 ft) overhead door, about 915 mm
counter height, and car lengths. Then rebuild.
